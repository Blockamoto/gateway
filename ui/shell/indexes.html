      Your local knowledge
      Coverage and sharing explained
      Each index reports its own retained instance and coverage. Coverage records completed work within the stated range. A retained result and its checkpoint are distinct from keeping its original source blocks. Checkpoints are rechecked against the selected chain before resume.
      Publishing freezes the displayed checkpoint and its records for peer sharing. Later work stays private until you publish again. Network service also requires the separate Serve data setting. Withdraw publication to stop sharing without deleting local records.
      

      Available providers
      Bitcoin Core and Ord are optional. A provider being configured, queryable or reachable does not imply complete historical coverage or permission to share its results.
      

      Inspect peer index claims
      Peer record exchange is not supported for this index in this release. Inspect local evidence and providers in Data sources.
      These buttons send explicit network requests to already-negotiated Gateway peers. Peers can observe the request. Claim inspection contacts at most four known peers and does not start discovery.
