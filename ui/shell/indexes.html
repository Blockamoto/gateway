    Rules and dependencies
      A definition describes what answers mean; it does not prove this node can answer them. Provider and retained-instance status appear below. Unsupported historical compatibility profiles remain unavailable and never rewrite Bitmap base results.
      Machine-readable definition
      

      Retained instances
      Coverage records completed work within the stated range. A retained result and its checkpoint are distinct from keeping its original source blocks. Checkpoints are rechecked against the selected chain before resume.
      Publishing freezes the displayed checkpoint and its records for peer sharing. Later work stays private until you publish again. Network service also requires the separate Serve data setting. Withdraw publication to stop sharing without deleting local records.
      

      Find a Bitmap district
      Enter the exact district number, without .bitmap or leading zeros. Lookup reads retained derived records; it does not build an index or fetch source blocks. Exact lookup may inspect the committed history and take longer than the bounded preview. A missing local result does not prove no claim exists.
      

      Inspect retained results
      Queries inspect retained records newest first, within a bounded window of at most 512 block batches. They do not start a build or fetch from the network.
      Result JSON and evidence
      

      Available providers
      Bitcoin Core and Ord are optional. A provider being configured, queryable or reachable does not imply complete historical coverage or permission to share its results.
      

      Inspect peer index claims
      These buttons send explicit network requests to already-negotiated Gateway peers. Peers can observe the request. Claim inspection contacts at most four known peers and does not start discovery. The preview checks one published block's internal commitments; it is not Bitcoin replay and does not store or import the result.
